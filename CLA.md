# Morgan Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0 — draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Have it reviewed before
accepting outside contributions.

Thank you for contributing to Morgan (this repository, `heyclaude`). This
agreement says what you grant when you do. It is modeled on the Apache Software
Foundation's Individual Contributor License Agreement.

**Why it exists.** Morgan is released under the GNU General Public License,
version 3. Its owner also offers it under other licenses, including commercial
ones, and uses that to fund the work. That is only possible if the owner holds
the right to license *every* part of Morgan, including your contribution. You
keep the copyright in what you write; this agreement gives the owner the license
needed to keep offering Morgan both ways.

**"Owner"** means Jeff Moore, and any person or entity to whom Jeff Moore
assigns the rights in Morgan.

**"You"** means the individual who submits a Contribution and agrees to this
agreement.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship — code, documentation,
designs, or anything else — that You intentionally submit to the Owner for
inclusion in Morgan, by any means: a pull request, a patch, a comment, an
email, or otherwise. Something You clearly mark "Not a Contribution" when You
submit it is not one.
